Fresher Guides

Understand your new environment.

University can feel confusing at first. These guides explain the important things you need to know.

📝 Admission & Clearance

  • Keep your admission documents safe.
  • Follow your institution's official instructions.
  • Complete acceptance procedures when required.
  • Prepare documents needed for clearance.
  • Complete school and departmental registration.
  • Keep copies of important documents.

📚 Academic Survival

  • Understand your course units.
  • Attend lectures consistently.
  • Learn how your institution calculates GPA/CGPA.
  • Keep track of assignments and tests.
  • Start preparing for examinations early.
  • Ask lecturers or academic advisers when confused.

🏫 Campus Life

  • Learn the important locations on campus.
  • Understand your faculty and department structure.
  • Join activities that genuinely interest you.
  • Build healthy friendships.
  • Respect campus rules.
  • Be careful with your personal information and belongings.

🛏️ Hostel & Accommodation

  • Understand your accommodation options.
  • Check location and transportation.
  • Know what items are allowed.
  • Keep valuables secure.
  • Respect roommates and neighbours.
  • Know emergency contacts and campus security channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions every fresher asks.

Quick answers to common university questions.

A course unit is a measure of the academic weight assigned to a course. The number of units can affect how the course contributes to your GPA.
GPA means Grade Point Average. It represents your academic performance for a particular semester or period, based on the grading system used by your institution.
CGPA means Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents your cumulative academic performance across the semesters included in the calculation.
University can be challenging, but good organization, consistent study habits, asking for help and managing your time can make the transition easier.
Student organizations can help you meet people, develop skills and participate in activities. Choose communities that genuinely interest you and respect your institution's rules.
Use your institution's official website, verified official social channels, student portal and authorized school communication channels for important information.
